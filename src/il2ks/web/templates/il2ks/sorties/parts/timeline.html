A run of ground kills is one folded row; the rows are paginated (20 a page, ?page_timeline=, OQ-96), so a sortie with hundreds of events stays readable and light. Context: timeline_page (a Page of rows), timeline_param, detail.timeline_events (all stored events). Rows: sortie_view.TimelineRow, rendered by timeline_table.html.
