Time is the flight's own clock (since spawn) with the real time of day beside it. A run of ground kills is one folded row; past the first rows the rest sits in a details element ("show all"), so a sortie with hundreds of events stays readable and light. Context: detail.timeline (the first rows), detail.timeline_more, detail.timeline_events (all stored events). Rows: sortie_view.TimelineRow, rendered by timeline_table.html. Hit rows (kinds hit_given / hit_taken) show the damage of a burst of hits in the last column, with the ammunition matched to the burst; sorties processed before these rows existed simply have none. {% endcomment %}
